Questions people ask

How does social media integration work in the Starter package?

We add links to your social profiles on your page, set up share previews so your site looks good when posted on WhatsApp, Facebook or LinkedIn, and add easy share buttons. You send us your profile links and we handle the rest during setup.

Is the domain name really included?

Yes. We register your domain name and handle all the technical setup (DNS, email routing, SSL and hosting connection) so your site goes live without you touching a thing.

What happens after the 1-year package ends?

After 12 months, you can renew your annual hosting and maintenance retainer at a preferential rate, or take full ownership of the source code with zero lock-in.

Can I upgrade between packages later?

Yes! Many clients start with the Micro-Starter or Standard package and seamlessly upgrade to our Stripe store or AI assistant tiers as their revenue grows.

What are the payment terms?

To secure your project slot, we require a 50% initial deposit, 25% upon launch, and the remaining 25% structured across the retainer period (or flexible monthly options available).
